01. About Me

I've spent two decades transforming complex technical problems into products people enjoy. From designing touch-responsive animations for Microsoft's first tablet OS to turning living rooms into multiplayer arcades with nothing but a TV and a few phones, the thread through my career is making sophisticated technology feel simple for customers.

At Amazon, I create gaming experiences that are fun, engaging, and effortless to dive into, removing the barriers that keep people from playing together. Before that, I owned personalization and engagement for Prime Gaming, shipping recommendation engines and A/B-tested features that measurably moved retention and conversion.

I'm currently investing in applied AI/ML, with recent certifications in deep learning, RAG, and generative AI from Stanford, DeepLearning.AI, and AWS.
