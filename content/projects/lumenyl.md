A dependency-free Node.js web app for benchmarking whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini / Google AI Overviews, and Copilot can discover, understand, trust, extract, and cite a web page. Produces scored GEO/AEO readiness reports across crawler access, answer quality, structured data, retrieval performance, authority, and emerging AI standards.
